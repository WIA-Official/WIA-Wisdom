# Hadisi | Hadith (حديث)

> Maganganu da Al'adun Annabi Muhammadu

---

## Bayani Gaba Ɗaya | Overview

Hadisi (Larabci: حديث, jam'i: ahadith) tarin maganganu ne, da ayyuka, da amincewar Annabi Muhammadu (tsira da amincin Allah su tabbata a gare shi), wanda Musulmi ke girmamawa a matsayin babban tushen shari'ar addini da jagorancin ɗabi'a tare da Alƙur'ani. Alhali Alƙur'ani maganar Allah ce kai tsaye, Hadisi yana wakiltar Sunnar (hanyar) Annabi—misali mai rai na yadda ake amfani da koyarwar Alƙur'ani.

Sahabban Annabi ne suka isar da Hadisi da baki, sannan aka rubuta shi tun daga farkon shekarun 700 Miladiyya. An tattara mafi inganci daga cikin tarin Hadisai a ƙarni na 9 da na 10 ta hannun malamai waɗanda suka ƙirƙiri tsauraran hanyoyin tabbatar da sahihancinsu.

## Tsarin Hadisi | Structure of Hadith

### Sassa:
| Sashe | Larabci | Bayani |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Isnadi** | إسناد | Jerin masu riwaya |
| **Matni** | متن | Ainihin rubutu/abin da ke ciki |

### Misali:
```
ISNAD: "Abu Hurayrah ya ruwaito cewa Manzon Allah ya ce..."
MATN: "Ana auna ayyuka da niyya, kuma kowane mutum zai sami 
       sakamako gwargwadon niyyarsa."
```

## Rabe-raben Hadisi | Classification of Hadith

### Bisa Ingancinsa:
| Nau'i | Larabci | Bayani |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Sahih** | صحيح | Lafiyayye/tabbatacce - mafi girman aminci |
| **Hasan** | حسن | Mai kyau - masu riwaya amintattu masu rauni kaɗan a ƙwaƙwalwa |
| **Da'if** | ضعيف | Mai rauni - aibi a cikin jerin masu riwaya ko a rubutu |
| **Mawdu** | موضوع | Ƙirƙirarre - ana ƙin shi a matsayin ƙarya |

### Bisa Asali:
| Nau'i | Bayani |
|------|-------------|
| **Hadisi Nabawi** | Hadisin Annabi |
| **Hadisi Ƙudsi** | Hadisi mai tsarki - kalmomin Allah ta bakin Annabi |

> "Hadisi Ƙudsi maganganun Annabi Muhammadu ne waɗanda Allah Maɗaukakin Sarki ya saukar masa. Ba kamar mafi yawan Hadisai waɗanda Hadisan Annabi ne ba, ikonsu ba ya komawa ga Annabi sai ga Maɗaukaki."

## Shahararrun Hadisai | Famous Hadith

### A kan Niyya:
> "Ana auna ayyuka da niyya, kuma kowane mutum zai sami sakamako gwargwadon niyyarsa." (Bukhari)

### A kan 'Yan'uwantaka:
> "Ɗayanku ba ya cika imani har sai ya so wa ɗan'uwansa abin da yake so wa kansa." (Bukhari, Muslim)

### A kan Ilimi:
> "Neman ilimi wajibi ne ga kowane Musulmi." (Ibn Majah)

### A kan Tausayi:
> "Allah Mai tausasawa ne, kuma yana son tausasawa a cikin kowane al'amari." (Bukhari, Muslim)

### A kan Halaye:
> "Mafi alherin ku su ne waɗanda suka fi kyawawan halaye." (Bukhari)

## Matsayi a Shari'ar Musulunci | Role in Islamic Law

### Tushen Fikihun Musulunci:
1. **Alƙur'ani** - Tushe na farko, maganar Allah
2. **Hadisi/Sunna** - Tushe na biyu, misalin Annabi
3. **Ijma'i** - Yarjejeniyar malamai
4. **Ƙiyasi** - Hujja ta kwatanci

> "Hadisai a taƙaice maganganu ne, da ayyuka, da aikace-aikacen Muhammadu. Sun bambanta da Alƙur'ani amma suna ba da taswirar hanyoyin yin rayuwa mai tsarki bisa rayuwar Annabi da kansa."

## Ilimin Hadisi | Science of Hadith

### Binciken Ingancin Hadisi:
Malamai sun ƙirƙiri tsauraran hanyoyi don tabbatar da sahihancin hadisi:

| Hanya | Manufa |
|--------|---------|
| **Nazarin Isnadi** | Tabbatar da jerin riwaya |
| **Tarihin Mai Riwaya** | Duba amincin masu isar da hadisi |
| **Nazarin Matni** | Bincika daidaiton abin da ke ciki |
| **Kwatantawa** | Haɗa da sauran hadisai don tabbatarwa |

### Ma'aunin Tantance Mai Riwaya:
- Adalci (adalah)
- Daidaito (dabt)
- Jerin da ba ya katsewa (ittisal)
- Rashin aibi ɓoyayye (salama min shudhdh)
- Rashin saɓani (salama min illa)

## Al'adar Hadisai Arba'in | Forty Hadith Tradition

> "Akwai riwaya da yawa game da falalar haddace hadisai arba'in, da kiyaye su, da koyar da su."
