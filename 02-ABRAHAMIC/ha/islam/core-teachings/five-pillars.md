# Ginshiƙai Biyar na Musulunci | The Five Pillars of Islam (أَرْكَانُ الْإِسْلَامِ)

> Ayyukan ibada guda biyar na asali waɗanda suke tushen rayuwar Musulmi

---

## Bayani Gaba Ɗaya | Overview

Ginshiƙai Biyar na Musulunci (Larabci: أَرْكَانُ الْإِسْلَامِ, *Arkān al-Islām*) su ne ayyuka biyar na asali waɗanda ake sa ran kowane Musulmi ya cika a tsawon rayuwarsa. Annabi Muhammadu (tsira da amincin Allah su tabbata a gare shi) ya siffanta Musulunci a matsayin wanda "aka gina shi a kan abubuwa biyar" (Sahihul Bukhari, Sahih Muslim), wanda ke nuna cewa waɗannan ginshiƙai su ne muhimmin tsarin addini.

Ginshiƙan su ne:
1. **Shahada** (شَهَادَة) — Shaidawar Imani
2. **Salla** (صَلَاة) — Addu'a ta ibada
3. **Zakka** (زَكَاة) — Bayar da sadaka ta wajibi
4. **Azumi** (صَوْم) — Yin azumi
5. **Hajji** (حَجّ) — Aikin hajji

Waɗannan ayyuka suna haɗa imani da aiki, suna nuna cewa imani a Musulunci ba amincewa ta hankali kawai ba ne, sai dai ibada ce da ake aiwatarwa da jiki. Kowane ginshiƙi yana ƙarfafa Tauhidi (kaɗaitakar Allah) kuma yana gina cikakken tsari na rayuwar ruhi, ta zamantakewa da ta ɗabi'a.

---
## Ginshiƙi na Farko: Shahada | The First Pillar: Shahada
### Shaidawar Imani | Declaration of Faith
> **أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ**
"Ina shaida cewa babu abin bautawa da gaskiya sai Allah, kuma ina shaida cewa Muhammadu Manzon Allah ne."

---

Shahada ita ce babbar shaidawar imanin Musulunci. Ta ƙunshi shaidu biyu:

1. **Tauhidi** — "Babu abin bautawa da gaskiya sai Allah" tana tabbatar da cikakkiyar kaɗaitakar Allah
2. **Risala** — "Muhammadu Manzon Allah ne" tana tabbatar da annabcin Muhammadu

Mutum yana zama Musulmi ta hanyar karanta Shahada da Larabci da gaskiya, tare da fahimta da cikakken imani. Ana kuma karanta wannan shaidawa a lokacin kiran salla (adhan), a cikin salla kanta, da kuma a lokuta masu muhimmanci na rayuwa.

---
## Ginshiƙi na Biyu: Salla | The Second Pillar: Salat
### Sallar Ibada | Ritual Prayer

Salla ita ce addu'ar ibada da ake yi sau biyar a rana a lokutan da aka shimfiɗa, ana fuskantar Ka'aba a Makka. Ita ce mafi bayyanar nuni na imanin Musulmi.

**Sallolin Yau da Kullum Biyar:**

| Salla | Larabci | Lokaci |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Asuba (Fajr)** | فَجْر | Wayewar gari (kafin fitowar rana) |
| **Azahar (Dhuhr)** | ظُهْر | Tsakar rana (bayan rana ta wuce tsakiyar sama) |
| **La'asar (Asr)** | عَصْر | Rana ta yamma |
| **Magariba (Maghrib)** | مَغْرِب | Faɗuwar rana (kai tsaye bayan rana ta faɗi) |
| **Isha'i (Isha)** | عِشَاء | Dare (bayan jan hwaihwayin yamma ya ɓace) |

**Abubuwan da Salla Ta Ƙunsa:**

1. **Niyya** (nufi) — Yin niyyar yin salla
2. **Kabbara** — Ɗaga hannuwa da faɗin "Allahu Akbar" (Allah ne Mafi Girma)
3. **Alƙiyama (Qiyam)** — Tsayawa da karanta Suratul Fatiha da sauran ayoyi
4. **Ruku'u** — Rusunawa da hannuwa a kan gwiwoyi
5. **Sujada** — Sunkuyawa har goshi ya taɓa ƙasa
6. **Tahiyya (Tashahhud)** — Zama da karanta shaidawar imani
7. **Sallama (Taslim)** — Kammalawa da "Assalamu alaikum" (Aminci ya tabbata a gare ku)

---
## Ginshiƙi na Uku: Zakka | The Third Pillar: Zakat
### Sadaka ta Wajibi | Obligatory Almsgiving

Zakka ita ce bayar da wani kaso na dukiyar mutum ga masu bukata, wanda wajibi ne. Ita ibada ce kuma tsarin jin daɗin jama'a ne.

**Muhimman Abubuwa:**

- **Adadi**: Gabaɗaya kashi 2.5% (1/40) na dukiyar da aka tara kuma ta shafe shekara guda ta wata
- **Nisabi**: Mafi ƙarancin adadin dukiya kafin zakka ta zama wajibi
- **Masu karɓa**: Alƙur'ani ya bayyana rukunoni takwas (9:60):
  1. Matalauta (al-fuqara)
  2. Miskinai (al-masakin)
  3. Masu karɓar zakka
  4. Waɗanda ake lallashin zukatansu
  5. Waɗanda ke cikin bauta ('yanta bayi)
  6. Masu bashi
  7. Cikin hanyar Allah
  8. Matafiya masu bukata

Zakka tana tsarkake dukiya kuma tana tsarkake zuciya daga kwaɗayi. Tana tabbatar da zagayawar dukiya a cikin al'umma kuma tana taimaka wa marasa galihu.

---
## Ginshiƙi na Huɗu: Azumi | The Fourth Pillar: Sawm
### Azumin Ramadan | Fasting in Ramadan

Azumi shi ne wajibcin yin azumi a watan Ramadan, wata na tara a kalandar wata ta Musulunci. A cikin wannan wata, Musulmi suna kamewa daga abinci, abin sha, shan taba, da saduwar aure daga wayewar gari (fajr) har zuwa faɗuwar rana (maghrib).

**Muhimmancinsa na Ruhi:**

- **Taƙawa**: Azumi yana renon tsoron Allah da horon kai
- **Godiya**: Hana kai na ɗan lokaci yana sabunta godiya ga ni'imomin Allah
- **Tausayi**: Jin yunwa yana haifar da haɗin kai da talakawa cikin jin zafinsu
- **Alƙur'ani**: Ramadan shi ne watan da aka fara saukar da Alƙur'ani a cikinsa

**Rangwame**: Tsofaffi, marasa lafiya, masu ciki, masu shayarwa, masu jinin haila, da matafiya za su iya samun rangwame, tare da tanadin rama kwanakin da aka rasa ko bayar da diyya (fidya).

**Darare na Musamman:**
- **Lailatul Ƙadari** (Daren Girma): Daren da aka saukar da Alƙur'ani, wanda ya fi watanni dubu (Sura ta 97)

---
## Ginshiƙi na Biyar: Hajji | The Fifth Pillar: Hajj
### Aikin Hajji a Makka | Pilgrimage to Mecca

Hajji shi ne aikin ziyara na shekara-shekara zuwa Makka wanda wajibi ne kowane Musulmi mai ƙarfin jiki da na kuɗi ya yi shi aƙalla sau ɗaya a rayuwarsa. Yana faruwa a watan Zulhijja (wata na 12 a kalandar Musulunci).

**Muhimman Ayyuka:**

1. **Harama (Ihram)**: Shiga yanayin tsarki na ibada; sanya fararen tufafi masu sauƙi waɗanda ke kawar da bambancin aji, ƙasa, da al'ada

2. **ɗawafi (Tawaf)**: Kewaya Ka'aba sau bakwai a gaba da agogo

3. **Sa'ayi (Sa'i)**: Tafiya sau bakwai tsakanin tuddan Safa da Marwa, don tunawa da neman ruwa da Hajaratu ta yi

4. **Arafa (Arafat)**: Tsayawa cikin addu'a a filin Arafa (ɗaya daga cikin ayyukan da suka fi muhimmanci)

5. **Muzdalifa (Muzdalifah)**: Tattara tsakuwa da yin addu'a bayan faɗuwar rana

6. **Jifan Jamarat (Rami al-Jamarat)**: Jifan ginshiƙai uku masu wakiltar Shaiɗan da tsakuwa

7. **Layya (Qurbani)**: Yanka dabba, don tunawa da shirin Annabi Ibrahim na yanka ɗansa

8. **ɗawafin Ifada (Tawaf al-Ifadah)**: Kewaya Ka'aba na ƙarshe

**Muhimmancinsa na Ruhi:**

Hajji babbar alama ce ta haɗin kan Musulmai. Miliyoyin mahajjata daga kowace ƙasa, jinsi, da matakin zamantakewa suna tsayawa tare a matsayin daidai da juna a gaban Allah. Yana tunawa da gwaje-gwajen Annabi Ibrahim, Hajaratu, da Annabi Isma'ila, yana haɗa Musulmai da gadonsu na Ibrahim.

## Ra'ayoyin Sunni da Shi'a | Sunni and Shia Perspectives

Ko da yake Musulmin Sunni da na Shi'a suna tarayya a Ginshiƙai Biyar, Musulunci na Shi'a yana da ƙarin ayyuka na wajibi:

**"Ayyuka Goma" na Shi'a (Furu'ud-Din):**
1-5. Ginshiƙai Biyar (ana tarayya da Sunni)
6. **Khumusi (Khums)** — Haraji na 20% a kan wasu kuɗaɗen shiga
7. **Jihadi** — Ƙoƙari a hanyar Allah
8. **Amr bil Ma'ruf** — Umarni da aikata alheri
9. **Nahi 'an al-Munkar** — Hani daga aikata mummuna
10. **Tawalla** — Son iyalan Annabi
11. (Wasu suna ƙarawa) **Tabarra** — Barranta daga maƙiyan iyalan Annabi

## Haɗin Ginshiƙan Juna | The Unity of the Pillars

Ginshiƙai Biyar suna aiki tare a matsayin tsari guda ɗaya:

- **Shahada** tana kafa tushen imani da Allah Ɗaya
- **Salla** tana kiyaye alaƙar yau da kullum da Allah ta hanyar ibada mai tsari
- **Zakka** tana shimfiɗa ibada a kwance don kula da al'umma
- **Azumi** yana tsarkake rai ta hanyar keɓewa ta ruhi a kowace shekara
- **Hajji** yana haɗa al'ummar Musulmi ta duniya a ibada ta gamayya

Tare, suna magana kan dukkan fannonin rayuwar ɗan Adam: imani, ibada, nauyin zamantakewa, horon kai, da asalin al'umma.

## Manyan Madogara | Primary Sources

### Hadisi kan Ginshiƙai Biyar | Hadith on the Five Pillars
> "An gina Musulunci a kan abubuwa biyar [ginshiƙai]: shaidawa cewa babu abin bautawa da gaskiya sai Allah kuma Muhammadu Manzon Allah ne, tsayar da salla, bayar da zakka, aikin hajji zuwa Gida, da azumin Ramadan." (Sahihul Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)

- **Salla**: Sura ta 2:43, 11:114, 17:78
- **Zakka**: Sura ta 9:60, 2:43, 2:177
- **Azumi**: Sura ta 2:183-185
- **Hajji**: Sura ta 2:196-197, 3:97, 22:27-29

## Aiwatarwa a Rayuwa | Practice Application

Ginshiƙai Biyar suna ba da hikima ta zahiri ga rayuwar yau da kullum:

1. **Fara da shaidawa** — Bayyanar manufa da asali
2. **Kafa tsari** — Ibada ta ruhi akai-akai tana tsara rayuwa
3. **Ba da kyauta hannu sake** — Ana tsarkake dukiya ta hanyar rabawa
4. **Yi kamewa** — Horon kai yana ƙarfafa halayya
5. **Nemi al'umma** — Ana rayuwar imani a cikin dangantaka da wasu
