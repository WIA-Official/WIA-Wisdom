# Sallah (Sallar Musulunci) | Salat (Islamic Prayer)

> Sallolin Yau da Kullum Guda Biyar

---

## Bayani Gabaɗaya | Overview

Sallah (Larabci: ٱلصَّلَاةُ, aṣ-Ṣalāh), wadda a wasu wurare ake kira Namaz, ita ce aikin ibada ta yau da kullum a Musulunci, wadda ta ƙunshi jerin sallolin ibada da ake yi a lokutan da aka shata a kowace rana. Sallah ɗaya ce daga cikin rukunan Musulunci guda biyar, kuma ginshiƙin ibada ce ga Musulmi.

Babban manufar sallah ita ce ta zama hanyar sadarwa da Allah. Sauran abubuwan da ake jaddadawa sun haɗa da tsarkake zuciya, kusantar Allah, da ƙarfafa imani.

---

## Sallolin Yau da Kullum Guda Biyar | The Five Daily Prayers

> "Musulmi suna yin sallah aƙalla sau biyar a rana, ana kiran sallolinsu na farilla (wajibi) da Asubah (kafin wayewar gari), Azahar (tsakar rana), La'asar (ƙarshen la'asar), Magariba (lokacin faɗuwar rana), da Isha'i (lokacin dare), kullum suna fuskantar Ka'aba."

| Sallah | Larabci | Lokaci | Ma'ana |
|--------|--------|------|---------|
| **Asubah (Fajr)** | الفجر | Kafin wayewar gari | Alfijir |
| **Azahar (Dhuhr)** | الظهر | Bayan tsakar rana | Tsakar rana |
| **La'asar (Asr)** | العصر | Ƙarshen la'asar | La'asar |
| **Magariba (Maghrib)** | المغرب | Lokacin faɗuwar rana | Faɗuwar rana |
| **Isha'i (Isha)** | العشاء | Lokacin dare | Dare |

---

## Yadda Ake Yin Sallah | How Prayer is Performed

> "Waɗannan sallolin sun ƙunshi raka'o'i, sun haɗa da wasu matsayoyin jiki na musamman, karatun Alƙur'ani, da addu'o'i daga Sunnah, kuma ana yin su ana fuskantar inda Ka'aba take a Makka (alƙibla)."

### Matsayoyi Huɗu: | The Four Postures

| Matsayi | Larabci | Bayani |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Tsayuwa** | Qiyam | Karatun Alƙur'ani |
| **Ruku'u** | Ruku | Tanƙwarawa a kugu |
| **Sujada** | Sujud | Goshi a ƙasa |
| **Zama** | Juloos | Tsakanin sujadoji |

### Muhimman Karance-karance: | Key Recitations

> "Bayan tsayuwa, masu ibada su koma ruku'u, su karanta 'subhana rabbiyal azim' ('Tsarki ya tabbata ga Ubangijina, Mai girma'). Bayan haka sai sujada tare da karanta 'subhana rabbiyal a'laa' ('Tsarki ya tabbata ga Ubangijina, Maɗaukaki')."

---

## Shiri | Preparation

### Alwala (Wanke-wanken Ibada): | Wudu (Ritual Washing)

> "Koyarwar Musulunci ta wajabta wa Musulmi yin wanke-wanken ibada na hannaye, ƙafafu, damtsuna, da ƙwauri, wanda ake kira alwala (Wudu), kafin yin sallah."

### Sharuɗɗa: | Requirements

- Tsabtataccen jiki (alwala)
- Tsabtataccen wuri
- Tsabtataccen tufafi
- Suturar kamala
- Fuskantar alƙibla (inda Makka take)

---

## Muhimmancin Ruhi | Spiritual Significance

> "Yin sallah an yi nufin ta zama aiki mai zurfin ruhi wanda ke haɗa dukkan Musulmi da Allah da kuma juna. Sanya sallolin a lokuta biyar daban-daban a kowace rana yana kafa tsari wanda Musulmi za su iya tsara ranarsu da shi."

### Manufofi: | Purposes

| Manufa | Amfani |
|---------|---------|
| **Sadarwa** | Haɗin kai kai tsaye da Allah |
| **Tsarkakewa** | Tsarkake zuciya |
| **Kusanci** | Kusantar Allah |
| **Horo** | Tsara yini |
| **Al'umma** | Zama tsintsiya madaurinki ɗaya da dukkan Musulmi |

> "Ana imani cewa rai yana buƙatar sallah da kusanci ga Allah domin ya ci gaba da samun abinci da lafiya."

---

## Aiki a Zamanin Yau | Modern Practice

> "Ci gaban fasaha ya sauƙaƙa kiyaye yin sallah akai-akai. Bidiyoyin koyarwa suna ba sababbin Musulmi da yara damar koyon yadda ake sallah; lokutan sallah da aka ƙiyasta ta hanyar kimiyya suna ba Musulmi hanya mafi sauƙi ta sanin lokutan da za su yi sallah; manhajojin wayar hannu sun ƙunshi Alƙur'ani na zamani da masu tuna lokaci ta atomatik waɗanda ke kunna kiran sallah (adhan) sau biyar a rana."
