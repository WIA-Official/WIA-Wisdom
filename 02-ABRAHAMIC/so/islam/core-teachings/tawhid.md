# Tawxiid | Tawhid (توحيد)

> Keliyaynta Ilaah: Caqiidada udub-dhexaadka u ah Islaamka

---

## Guudmar | Overview

Tawxiid, oo macnaheedu yahay "midayn" ama "ka dhigid mid," waa mabda'a keliyaynta Ilaah (monotheism) ee Islaamka. Waa fikradda udub-dhexaadka ah ee diinta, tan ugu muhiimsan, waxaana ku dhisan dhammaan hoggaansanaanta diineed ee Muslimka. Si cad ayay u xaqiijisaa in Ilaah yahay mid aan loo qaybin karin (ahad) iyo mid keliya (waaxid).

Tawxiid waa qodobka ugu horreeya ee shahaadada Muslimka. Sida ay sheegto cilmiga caqiidada Islaamka, waa mabda'a aasaasiga ah ee go'aamiya sida aad u fahmayso wax kasta oo dhan: caabudaadda, akhlaaqda, sayniska, xiriirrada, ujeeddada, iyo xaqiiqada qudheeda.

---

## Shahaadada | The Shahada

> "Tawxiid waa qodobka ugu horreeya ee shahaadada hoggaansanaanta Muslimka. Qaybta koowaad ee shahaadada Islaamka (shahaadah) waa caddaynta rumaysadka keliyaynta Ilaah."

### Caddaynta | The Declaration:
**Carabi:** لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ

**Tarjumaad:** "Ma jiro ilaah xaq u leh in la caabudo illaa Allaah, Muxammad-na waa Rasuulkiisa."

> "Shahaadadu waa muujin af ah oo Tawxiidka ah, taasoo xaqiijinaysa rumaysadka keliyaynta Allaah iyo nabinimada Muxammad (NNKH)."

---

## Saddexda Qaybood ee Tawxiidka | Three Categories of Tawhid

> "Culimada Islaamku waxay Tawxiidka u kala saareen saddex nooc oo is-xiga."

### 1. Tawxiidka Rubuubiyadda (Sayidnimada) | Tawhid al-Rububiyyah (Lordship)
> "Tawxiidka Rubuubiyaddu waxa uu tilmaamayaa keliyaynta Ilaah marka loo eego sayidnimadiisa koonka. Waxa uu xooga saaraa in Allaah yahay abuuraha keliya, rizqi-bixiyaha iyo maamulaha wax walba."

### 2. Tawxiidka Uluuhiyadda (Caabudaadda) | Tawhid al-Uluhiyyah (Worship)
> "Tawxiidka Uluuhiyaddu waxa uu xaqiijinayaa keliyaynta Allaah ee caabudaadda. Muslimiintu waxay rumaysan yihiin in dhammaan falalka caabudaadda, du'aada iyo hoggaansanaanta loo jiheeyo Allaah keligiis, iyada oo la dididdan yahay wax kasta oo lamaanayn ama shuraako ah oo caabudaadda ku jira."

### 3. Tawxiidka Asmaa'ka iyo Sifaadka (Magacyada iyo Sifooyinka) | Tawhid al-Asma wa Sifat (Names and Attributes)
> "Tawxiidka Asmaa'ka iyo Sifaadku waxa uu tilmaamayaa gaarnimada magacyada iyo sifooyinka Allaah. Dhinacani waxa uu qirayaa in Allaah leeyahay sifooyin kaamil ah oo aan la mid ahayn."

---

## Shirki: Ka Soo Horjeedka Tawxiidka | Shirk: The Opposite of Tawhid

> "In wax kale ama qof kale loo nisbeeyo ilaahnimo waxa loo tixgeliyaa shirki, kaasoo ah dambi aan la cafiyi doonin haddii aan laga towbad keenin, sida ku timid Qur'aanka."

### Noocyada Shirki | Types of Shirk:
| Nooca | Sharaxaad |
|------|-------------|
| **Weyn (Shirk Akbar)** | Allaah oo shuraako lagu darsado caabudaadda |
| **Yar (Shirk Asghar)** | Shirki qarsoon (isla-muujin ku jirta caabudaadda) |
| **Qarsoon (Shirk Khafi)** | Noocyo qarsoon oo daacadnimo qaybsan ah |

### Culayska Dambigan | The Gravity:
Shirku waa dambiga kaliya ee Allaah aan cafiyi doonin haddii qofku dhinto isagoo aan ka towbad keenin (Qur'aanka 4:48).

---

## Habab Aqooneed | Scholarly Approaches

> "Fahamka macnaha 'tawxiid' waa mid ka mid ah arrimaha ugu muranka badan ee ka dhexeeya Muslimiinta. Culimada Islaamku waxay leeyihiin hababka kala duwan ee loo fahmo, kuwaas oo ay ka mid yihiin habka nassiga ah, habka caqiido-cilmiyeed, habka falsafadeed, iyo habka Suufiyadda iyo Cirfaaniga."

### Madaaris Caqiido | Theological Schools:
| Madrasadda | Xooggaynta |
|--------|----------|
| **Ash'ari** | Kalaam caqli ku dhisan oo leh xudduudo dhaqameed |
| **Maturidi** | Isu dheelitirka caqliga iyo waxyiga |
| **Athari** | Habka nassiga ah ee xarfiga ah |
| **Suufi/Cirfaani** | Midnimo waayo-aragnimo ah oo Ilaah lala leeyahay |

> "Taariikhda Islaamka oo dhan, waxaa soo baxay madaaris caqiido oo kala duwan, mid walbana wuxuu leeyahay habkiisa uu u fahmo magacyada iyo sifooyinka Allaah."

---

## Muhiimadda Nolosha Maalinlaha ah | Significance in Daily Life

> "Sida ay sheegto cilmiga caqiidada Islaamka, Tawxiid waa mabda'a aasaasiga ah ee go'aamiya sida aad u fahmayso wax kasta oo dhan: caabudaadda, akhlaaqda, sayniska, xiriirrada, ujeeddada, iyo xaqiiqada qudheeda. Dhammaan waxbarasho kasta oo Islaamka ah waxay ka soo burqataa Tawxiidka."

### Dhaqan-gelinta | Applications:
| Meel | Saameyn |
|------|-------------|
| **Caabudaad** | Salaad kasta waxaa loo jiheeyaa Allaah keligiis |
| **Akhlaaq** | Addeecidda amarrada Allaah |
| **Sharci** | Shareecadu waxay ka tarjumaysaa doonista rabbaaniga ah |
| **Sayniska** | Koonku waxa uu muujinayaa midnimada rabbaaniga ah |
| **Bulsho** | Sinnaan hortiisa Ilaah keliya |
